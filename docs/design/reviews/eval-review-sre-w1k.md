## Seam disagreements

- **Alarm vs resume (finding 1).** Section 6.2 ("pending = no `cell.outcome`") and section 3.1 step 4 ("nothing to do") describe the same question differently. Both are this one doc; the W0 section 14 text and ADR-0021 section 7 ("while cells are pending") are silent on the grading and archive phases.
- **R-100 condition 6 vs design row C7 (finding 2).** The ruling's premise is that no cell is pending after a finished stop; the design leaves never-launched cells unrecorded.
- No disagreement found with W0 rev 6.8 sections 11 and 14 on the codes (`HB-ALM-001..003`, exit 6) or with the R6.8b conditions, except finding 5 (a gap in how condition 2 is tested).

## Checked and sound

`--alarm-after` exit 6 fails closed (an erroring check never reads as "no alarm"); the stamp is touched first; `last_progress_at` is derived from the ledger tail (no new row); `bench-status/2` accepts `/1`; the free-lock case fires at once; the `-DryRun` plus stub `bench` test never shows a toast or touches the network; `powershell.exe` 5.1 is named; the topic lives only in a user variable; the runbook and wrapper paths are granted (R6.8b).
