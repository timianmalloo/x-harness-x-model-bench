Add `Scheduler.on_failure(callback)`. Each time a job raises, call every registered callback with `(job, exception)`, in the order they were registered.

A job is paused when any of those callbacks returns `False`. A job is also paused when it fails 3 times in a row. A paused job is not run again until `job.resume()` is called. `job.resume()` clears the pause and sets `job.failures` back to 0.

The `failures` counter keeps working as before.

Add tests for this to `test_schedule.py`, and keep the existing tests passing.
