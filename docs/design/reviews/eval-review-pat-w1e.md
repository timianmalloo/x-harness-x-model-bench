- "Specification predicates returning `Failure`" (a Notification, all items reported at once), `ast.literal_eval` for `variants.py`, and importing `runner.applicable` and `property.at_scale` instead of copying them are the right reuse rungs.
- Readiness codes 002, 003, 004, 005, 006, 007, 008, 009, 010, 011 map to W0 §11 rows as worded. Code 001 also covers "record exists but lacks the real-host probe", which W0 §2 rev 5 and §6 endorse ("fails HB-RDY-001").
