# W1-J multi-turn attempt: SRE review

Target: `docs/design/eval-multi-turn.md` on `design/eval-multi-turn` (08fabe34, 6b838ff2). Against W0 rev 6 (`f5808497`) s4, s11, s12; ADR-0015; R-87..R-98. Code read in the design's tree. RV-TA/RV-DS topics (test shape, ordering proofs) are not repeated.

Answers to the brief:
- Per-turn time, snapshot time, bytes and spend: yes, by default (`turn_seconds`, `duration_ms`, `files`, `bytes`, `usage`, all null-not-zero).
- Why turn 2 was not sent: **no** on the paths that matter (finding 1).
- Budget clock `turn == 1` (`engine.py:283`): right, one line, mutant M-CLOCK. But the operator's clock disagrees (finding 2).
- Spend summed over turns (`engine.py:663`): right for `acp_turn`; SPEND and `turn_usage` agree. `turn_ms` is last turn only (finding 6).
- `end_turn`-only: right (`COMPLETED_STOP_REASONS` at `engine.py:60`), but unrecorded (finding 1).
- `job_active_baseline`: a good idea, taken too early and counted once (finding 4).
